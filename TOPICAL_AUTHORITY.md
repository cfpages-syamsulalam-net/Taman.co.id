# Topical Authority — taman.co.id

Last reviewed: 2026-07-23 (Asia/Jakarta)

## Role and boundary

Hub pengetahuan lanskap Indonesia yang menghubungkan analisis tapak, desain, tanah, tanaman, air, hardscape, struktur taman, ekologi, pembangunan, perawatan, troubleshooting, dan pengadaan; route jasa lama tetap memiliki intent transaksi.

Primary audience: Pemilik rumah dan properti, pengelola fasilitas, arsitek lanskap, desainer, kontraktor, gardener, nursery, estimator, pengelola kawasan, dan pembaca yang perlu merencanakan taman tropis secara bertanggung jawab.

The domain boundary is editorial, not portfolio-wide exclusivity. Other owned domains may cover the same query, entity, or topic from the same or a different point of view. Cannibalization controls in this plan apply only among URLs on **taman.co.id**. Cross-domain similarity is not used to suppress a useful article.

Commercial boundary: neutral guides explain decisions and link to /kontak-kami only when a reader is ready for a quote or service conversation. Existing commercial routes retain transactional intent; no article should silently replace them.

Geographic boundary: Indonesia is the primary context. Local pages require real differences in climate, regulation, supply, operations, architecture, field data, or a documented case. City-name substitution alone is prohibited.

## Existing coverage and risks

Existing coverage is primarily commercial or generated rather than a curated educational layer.

- Sebanyak 3,904 halaman lokasi tampak dibuat dengan pertukaran nama daerah; halaman seperti ini tidak boleh dianggap topical authority tanpa bukti lokal substantif.
- Sebanyak 783 URL category/news pagination membengkakkan index dan dapat menyebarkan sinyal internal-link tanpa memberi jawaban unik.
- Layanan taman bercampur dengan kolam, carport, ornamen dinding, dan tebing; setiap route perlu role yang jelas agar tidak berebut query generik.
- Saran tanaman, pestisida, struktur, listrik luar ruang, dan kolam memerlukan sumber primer dan reviewer yang sesuai; jangan mengarang dosis, struktur, atau aturan.

The highest same-domain risk is creating a new informational page whose title, outline, and primary query duplicate an existing service route. New slugs therefore use decision-oriented editorial wording and must pass the one-query, outline, SERP-intent, entity-attribute, and title/slug similarity tests before publication.

## Evidence and editorial standards

- Prefer current Indonesian government, standards-body, regulator, accredited-lab, manufacturer technical, university/research, and recognized professional sources. Record access/version dates.
- Do not name clauses, dimensions, ratings, doses, prices, warranties, licensing requirements, or service areas until verified from a current primary source.
- Safety-critical structural, electrical, mechanical, chemical, biological, fire, accessibility, health, maritime, vehicle, and regulated-work content requires a competent reviewer and explicit stop conditions.
- Separate observed field evidence, sourced fact, calculation assumption, manufacturer claim, practitioner judgment, and editorial hypothesis.
- Calculators publish formulas, units, input limits, worked examples, version history, and cases requiring professional design.
- Case studies require a real project, permission, dated inputs, constraints, process, outcome, limitations, and original or properly licensed evidence. Never fabricate first-hand experience.
- Price content uses a dated model and cost drivers, never a timeless national promise. Commercial pages state scope, exclusions, proof, process, warranty conditions, and service coverage honestly.
- Original photos preserve consent and provenance. Diagrams label what is schematic. AI-created illustrations may explain concepts but cannot serve as proof of a product, defect, project, or test result.
- Every draft repeats the same-domain collision check against live routes, sitemap URLs, titles, H1s, likely outlines, and Search Console queries. Cross-domain matches are excluded from this cannibalization test.
- Do not mass-produce local pages. A local page must contain unique operational/service evidence and a materially different answer.

- Technical and safety claims have primary evidence and the required reviewer; prices and case results are dated and non-fabricated.
