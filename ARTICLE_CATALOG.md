## Scope

This catalog expands every parent topic in `TOPICAL_AUTHORITY.md` into eight mutually distinct briefs. It plans editorial assets; it does not authorize automatic publication, fabricate evidence, or replace the required audit of existing routes.

## Domain boundaries

- Same-domain anti-cannibalization applies only among pages on **taman.co.id**, including proposed articles and existing routes.
- Other owned domains may independently cover the same entity, query, or point of view. Such overlap is allowed and is not entered as cannibalization.
- Existing commercial routes retain transactional intent. Educational pages explain a reader job and link to /kontak-kami only when contextually useful.
- Indonesia is the default context. No location page is created by swapping place names; local substance requires verified local evidence.
- Safety, standards, price, licensing, service coverage, and case claims remain unverified until supported by current primary evidence.
